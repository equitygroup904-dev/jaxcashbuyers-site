Selling in Jacksonville the easy way

Listing a house in Jacksonville can mean months of showings, inspection repairs, appraisal problems and buyers whose loans fall through. All the while you keep paying the mortgage, taxes, insurance and utilities.

When you sell to Jax Cash Buyers, we buy the house ourselves with cash. We don't need a bank, an appraisal or a list of repairs. You get one fair offer, one visit, and a closing date that fits your life.

Why Jacksonville homeowners sell to us

  • As-is: roof leaks, old HVAC, fire damage, junk left behind, all fine.
  • No fees: no commission and we pay normal closing costs.
  • Fast or flexible: close in as little as 7 days, or give yourself a few months.
  • Local: we're based on Touchton Road and buy houses every week across Duval County.
