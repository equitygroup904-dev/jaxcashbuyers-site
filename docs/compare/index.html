Work the numbers

We won't pay full retail. We buy as-is and take on the repairs and the risk. But when you subtract commission, closing costs, repairs, and months of mortgage, taxes, insurance and utilities while a listed house sits, the gap is often much smaller than people expect.

When listing makes more sense

If your house is updated, you have time, and you don't mind showings, listing with a good local agent may net you more. We'll tell you that honestly. If speed, certainty or condition matter more, a cash sale is usually the better fit.
