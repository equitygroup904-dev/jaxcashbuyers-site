Who we are

Meet James Stallworth

Jax Cash Buyers buys houses directly from homeowners who need a fast, simple sale. That could be because of foreclosure, an inherited or probate property, a divorce, tired-landlord problems, or a house that needs more work than it's worth.

When we commit to buying your house, the whole team is accountable for doing what we said. Whether that means working with a lender, the city on code violations, or the title company, we push until the job is done and you have your money.
