Questions about the process

How long does the whole process take?

Most sellers get a written offer within 24 hours of the walkthrough. Closing can be as little as 7 days once title is clear, or on whatever date works for you.

Do I have to clean or fix anything first?

No. We buy as-is. Take what you want and leave the rest behind.

Who handles the paperwork and payoffs?

A local, neutral title company. They pay off your mortgage and any liens at closing and send you the rest.

What if I still owe on the house or I'm behind on payments?

That's common. The mortgage and any liens are paid off at closing from the sale. Call us and we'll talk through your numbers.

Am I locked in once I ask for an offer?

No. The offer is free and there's no obligation until you sign a purchase agreement.
